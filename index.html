Our Story

Built on Friendship. Inspired by Food. Rooted in Community.

Best Choice Catering began in 2015 with a love of cooking, preparing meals for church gatherings, and serving the community.

What started as a passion for bringing people together through great food quickly grew into a catering business loved for its delicious meals, warm personalities, and personal touch.

Today, Maria E. Rodriguez leads Best Choice Catering with a dedicated team committed to exceptional food and outstanding service for every occasion.

Our passion is simple: bringing people together, creating memorable experiences, and making every event special.

From our kitchen to your table, thank you for allowing us to be part of your special moments.

We’re proud to serve the High Desert and surrounding areas, and we look forward to serving you!

Whether you're hosting a family celebration, party, or special gathering, Best Choice Catering helps make the occasion memorable with delicious food and flexible cuisine options.

We offer Mexican, Italian, taco bar, BBQ, American, and more. Hablamos español.
